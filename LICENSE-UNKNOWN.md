# Licence: unknown

The original SAO STOIC and RED sources, by Roger Hauck and Jonathan Mark of
the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory, were distributed as part of the
DECUS VAX85C tape. They carry no copyright or licence notice, and their
licence terms are unknown.

The originals can currently be found at:

- <https://www.digiater.nl/openvms/decus/vax85c/saostoic/>, the files of
  the `SAOSTOIC` directory of the tape;
- <https://www.digiater.nl/openvms/decus/zips_vms_attributes/vax85c.zip>,
  the whole VAX85C tape as a zip archive that keeps the VMS file
  attributes.

`saostoic-text/` in this repository holds Unix-text copies of the
original text files.
